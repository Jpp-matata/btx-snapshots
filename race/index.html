How to join the race

Mine the community chain.
Point your rigs at a main-chain pool (e.g. btx-pool.com:3334) or solo-mine against your own main-chain node. Every GPU shrinks the gap.
Make your node refuse the branch.
On your node — it's reversible with reconsiderblock, and survives restarts:
btx-cli invalidateblock 8240c62e62b47fc675610908c03045c244de1dfc06246209830ba9d98468952c
Check you're on the right chain.
This must return 5ee6458a…:
btx-cli getblockhash 228146 → 5ee6458a5533bfbf74a1ff39bf6aa2517c1af59d4ba813898b2742638abebc14
Starting fresh? Use the community snapshot at github.com/Jpp-matata/btx-snapshots — it already refuses the branch. Running 0.34.12rc1? Use the maintainer's latest prerelease v0.34.12rc1-3ac718cd: it parks the branch by default.

Why it matters

The branch forked after block 228,145 and first appeared on 25 Sep, already 250 blocks behind the chain everyone was building. It's valid, and it has carried more total work than the community chain, so a brand-new node started from before the fork would simply follow it, undoing 4,300+ blocks of everyone's mining. BTX's rule is that nodes don't automatically reorganize deeper than 6 blocks, and the maintainer's new build enforces it and parks the branch by default. This isn't one person's call: pools, node operators, easyNode, the snapshots and the maintainer all refuse it. Two things finish it: nodes refusing the branch, and hashrate on the community chain until our total work passes the branch's.
